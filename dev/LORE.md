# Zone 12: lore (from the author, 25 Oct 2026)

## Background
- **2018:** the West's pressure is destabilising Russia, and its economy is suffering.
- **The revolution:**
  - A radical neo-communist movement forms, with several officials inside the government and military commanders conspiring with it.
  - A revolution breaks out west of Moscow, and several military units and assets go over to the separatists along with their commanders.
- **The ICBM site and the strikes:**
  - The separatists take an ICBM launch station. Officers on their side supply the launch keys and digital codes.
  - Nuclear missiles and several dirty uranium bombs are launched. How and why is still to be decided.
  - All of them are shot down before reaching any major target, Moscow above all.
- **The front:**
  - A front line forms, and it becomes a fairly conventional war: the Russian army against the less organised separatists.
  - The lines keep changing, with large grey zones in between.
  - Much of the fighting happens in and around the places where the nuclear and dirty-bomb debris came down.
- **2020 (the present):** Russia is making gains, but the separatists won't give up. The West will not help Russia or its government.

## How the current game already fits
- The player is in the Russian army: FOB, BTR-80 "121", Captain Morozov. The enemy is the separatists.
- The radiation basin, the radiation signs and the "elevated radiation" zones match fallout from intercepted warheads or dirty bombs.
- The lumber-yard separatists hiding from drones fit, and so does Sasha's letter ("Still mad I left to join the separatists?").
- Tushonka dated 2019 is consistent with 2020.
- The Mi-24 flyovers, the distant artillery and the smoke columns match a conventional front with grey zones.

## Characters
- **Sasha:** a separatist soldier at the lumber yard and one of the men the player kills there. His notebook on the cot in the cabin holds an unfinished letter home to his family (Mama, Papa, Grandma), who were angry that he joined the separatists. His story ends at the lumber yard; the payoff is the player realising they killed him.
